# Rin

> *A marginalized trainee Dreamwalker.*

Rin is a trainee at [[world/shadow-academy|Shadow Academy]], marked as "unqualified"—trauma too severe, unsuitable for training.

## Basic Information

- **Identity**: Shadow Academy trainee
- **[[systems/shadows|Shadow]]**: [[characters/echo|Echo]]
- **Training AI**: [[characters/eda|Eda]]

## Situation

Rin is marginal at the Academy:

- Pathologized ("trauma too severe")
- Only one friend
- Long dissociated from his own shadow

But Rin's "problem" may not be a problem at all. He's preserved a kind of primal state—that state where shadow and person are inseparable.

## Core

Rin's existence poses a question:

*Are those marked as "incomplete" truly incomplete?*
*Or have they preserved something that most people have already severed?*
