# Glossary

## Core Concepts

### [[systems/shadows|Shadow]]
The severed, suppressed parts of the self. Generated from dreams. Not something "bad"—something denied.

### [[systems/dream-space|Dream Space]]
The deep world where shadows dwell. Persists continuously; represents a person's core structure.

### [[systems/dreamwalking|Dreamwalker]]
A person trained to enter others' dream spaces and work with shadows.

### [[systems/shadow-disasters|Shadow Disaster]]
When suppressed shadows erupt collectively, forming regional emotional catastrophes.

## Locations

### [[world/resonance-city|Resonance City]]
The city where the story takes place. Exquisite, cold, controlled. People here have no shadows.

### [[world/shadow-academy|Shadow Academy]]
An institution floating above Resonance City. Trains Dreamwalkers; preserves dreams.

### [[world/the-remainder|The Remainder]]
A group within the Academy. They work normal jobs but do "real" Dreamwalker work in private.

## Skills

### Perception
Reading the emotional states of environments, objects, and people. Finding cracks.

### Submersion
Deep meditation; entering one's own dream space.

### Breach
Entering others' dream spaces through cracks.

### Negotiation
Dialoguing with shadows, soothing them, building relationships.

### Transmission
Making things from dream space affect or manifest in the real world.

## States

### Tamed
A shadow that has been controlled, domesticated. Obeys commands but has lost its wildness.

### Negotiated
A state of cooperative relationship with one's shadow. Acknowledging its existence, respecting its temperament.

### Primal
A state where shadow and person are inseparable. Closest to the original condition.

## Mechanisms

### Dream Censorship
The city's systematic dream filtering mechanism. Dreams filtered out → shadows cannot form.

### Integration
The Academy's shadow control program. Nominally "unifying shadow and person"; actually further taming.

### Cracks
Entry points into others' dream spaces. Appear when emotions stir.

## History

### [[world/history#the-blur|The Blur]]
A period of obscured history. Many things vanished during this time.

### [[world/history#the-echo-era|The Echo Era]]
The era after The Blur. Appears to be a golden age, but suppression was accumulating.
